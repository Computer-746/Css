😊 My Favourite Manhwa 😊

🔥 The 11th School Trip 🔥

About the Manhwa

The 11th School Trip is a BL manhwa that combines supernatural elements, mystery, action, suspense and romance. The story follows Gajun and Seonwoo during a mysterious school trip where they become caught in a terrifying time loop. Gajun repeatedly experiences Seonwoo's death and must uncover the truth behind the mysterious events in order to break the cycle. The story becomes more intense as the characters try to understand what is happening while facing danger and discovering more about their abilities.

Why I Like It

I like The 11th School Trip because of its interesting and unpredictable plot that keeps the readers curious about what will happen next. The mystery surrounding the school trip and the repeating time loop makes every chapter feel important because there are always new questions about what is actually happening and whether the characters will be able to escape the cycle. The combination of supernatural elements, danger and mystery makes the story exciting instead of making it only about romance.
Another thing I really like is the detailed and beautiful art style. The characters are visually appealing, and their facial expressions make their emotions easy to understand. The action scenes, supernatural moments and darker parts of the story are also drawn in a way that makes the atmosphere more intense and interesting.
I also love the relationship between Gajun and Seonwoo. Their romance develops naturally through the situations they experience together. As Gajun repeatedly tries to protect Seonwoo, their relationship slowly becomes deeper and more meaningful. Their cute interactions and the way they gradually become closer make their romance enjoyable without making it feel rushed or forced.
I also really like the friends in their group because they add comedy to the story. Even when the situation becomes serious, dangerous or emotional, their funny interactions bring some lightness to the story and make the characters more enjoyable. I like how the comedy balances the darker and more serious parts of the story.
What I especially enjoy is how the BL romance is connected with the main story. The romance does not completely take over the mystery and supernatural plot. Instead, their feelings and relationship develop while they are dealing with the dangerous events around them, giving the readers cute romantic moments while also reminding us that this is a BL story.
I also hope that Ah-Young and Lee Donghoon eventually become a side couple. I really like their interactions and think it would be cute to see their relationship develop alongside Gajun and Seonwoo. I hope the story gives them more moments together and lets their relationship naturally grow into a sweet side romance.

My Favourite Features

  • Interesting and unpredictable plot
  • Mystery and time-loop storyline
  • Supernatural elements and action
  • Detailed and beautiful art style
  • Cute and natural romance
  • Good chemistry between Gajun and Seonwoo
  • Funny friends and comedy in serious situations
  • BL romance
  • Hope for Ah-Young and Lee Donghoon as a side couple
